Why we're building this.

Pets are family, but their health lives in scattered paper, group chats, and memory. When it matters most, no one can find it.

The Wag Lab Co is a pet-care company on a simple bet: give every pet a health record its owner truly holds, and better care follows for the pet, the vet, and everyone building to help them.

We started with our own, Cotton and Esther, and the pile of vet visits and lab reports that came with loving them. Pawma is the record we wished we'd had.

Pawma is our first product. It won't be our last. The Wag Lab Co is building a family of tools for pet health.
